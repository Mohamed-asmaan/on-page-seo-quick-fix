# Privacy Policy for On Page SEO Quick Fix

**Last Updated:** [Date]

## Single Purpose Declaration

**On Page SEO Quick Fix** has a single, clear purpose: to analyze and provide SEO insights for web pages directly in the user's browser.

## Privacy Policy

### Data Collection

**On Page SEO Quick Fix does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing information.**

### How the Extension Works

1. **Local Analysis Only**: All SEO analysis is performed entirely within your browser on your local device.

2. **No Data Transmission**: The extension does not send any data to external servers, third-party services, or our servers.

3. **No Tracking**: We do not use tracking technologies, analytics, cookies, or any other methods to monitor your usage.

4. **No Storage**: The extension does not store any information about the pages you visit or the analysis results.

### Permissions Used

The extension requires the following permissions for its core functionality:

- **`activeTab`**: To access the current webpage for SEO analysis
- **`scripting`**: To execute analysis scripts on the current page
- **`tabs`**: To identify the active tab
- **`sidePanel`**: To display the analysis results in a side panel
- **`host_permissions: <all_urls>`**: To allow analysis on any website you visit

These permissions are used **exclusively** for the SEO analysis functionality and nothing else.

### Third-Party Services

This extension does not integrate with any third-party services, APIs, or external data processors.

### Children's Privacy

Our extension does not knowingly collect information from children. Since we collect no data, this is not applicable.
