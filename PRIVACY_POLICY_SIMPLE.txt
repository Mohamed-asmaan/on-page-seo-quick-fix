PRIVACY POLICY - On Page SEO Quick Fix

DATA COLLECTION: NONE
On Page SEO Quick Fix does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, browsing information, or website content.

HOW IT WORKS:
- All SEO analysis is performed locally in your browser
- No data is sent to external servers or third-party services
- No tracking, analytics, or cookies are used
- No information is stored about pages you visit

PERMISSIONS:
The extension uses minimal permissions (activeTab, scripting, tabs, sidePanel) exclusively for SEO analysis functionality. These permissions are required to:
- Access the current webpage for analysis
- Execute analysis scripts
- Display results in the side panel

THIRD-PARTY SERVICES: NONE
This extension does not integrate with any third-party services or APIs.

Your privacy is completely protected. All analysis happens on your device, and no data leaves your browser.
